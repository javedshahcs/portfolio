Expance Tracker

Wonder App is a Flutter-based mobile application designed to let users explore and discover fascinating wonders in a simple, elegant way. The app is built using Dart and relies on Isar, a fast and lightweight local NoSQL database, to store all wonder data directly on the device. This ensures the app works smoothly even without an internet connection, with quick loading and instant data access. One of the core features of the app is the ability to mark any wonder as a favorite with a single tap, and just as easily remove it from favorites whenever needed. All favorite selections are saved persistently in the Isar database, so they remain intact even after closing and reopening the app. Users can browse wonders in two different formats depending on their preference. The card view presents each wonder in a large, visually appealing layout with images and descriptions, ideal for a more immersive browsing experience. The list view, on the other hand, offers a compact and scrollable format that allows users to quickly skim through many wonders at once. A dedicated favorites section brings together all the wonders a user has liked, also viewable in both card and list formats. Overall, Wonder App combines clean design, offline reliability, and flexible viewing options to create a smooth and enjoyable user experience.
